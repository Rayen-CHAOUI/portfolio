Mobile platform

Wassalni

Intercity carpooling platform designed to reduce congestion, lower costs, and connect travelers across wilayas.

Flutter Node.js Express.js MongoDB

Project goal

  • Reduce traffic congestion and CO₂ emissions.
  • Offer an economical and ecological alternative to traditional transport.
  • Make communication between riders and drivers straightforward.

Core features

  • Sign-up and login with phone number.
  • Search and publish inter-wilaya trips.
  • Trip booking and payment.
  • Roadside assistance listings with partners.
  • Favorite trips and profile management.
  • Add one or more personal vehicles.
  • SOS with geolocation.

Advanced features

  • Secure payment system.
  • In-app messaging between users.
  • Ratings and reviews.
  • Real-time location tracking.
  • Push notifications and OTP validation.

Safety

  • Complete trip history.
  • Emergency alert system.
  • Identity and phone verification.
  • Driver background review.
